## Authentication Vulnerabilities

Simply put, authentication (proving who you are) is easy to understand but incredibly critical to secure. If your login system has a flaw, everything else is at risk.

When attackers break through authentication, they don't just get access to private data and features—they also get a perfect launching pad for even bigger attacks. Knowing how to find, exploit, and bypass these flaws is essential for fixing them.
